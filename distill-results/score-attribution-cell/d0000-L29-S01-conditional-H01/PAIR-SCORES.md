Nothing is pooled over prompts, layers, steps, branches or heads.

`c_f[i,j]` is the score pair f adds to the logit of query i and key j. Positive and negative scores are summed separately, so they cannot cancel:

- **attention-weighted** (`att`): each query-key score is weighted by the attention the query gives that key, summed over keys and queries, divided by the 10,800 queries. `+` adds only the positive scores, `−` only the negative ones.
- **every combination once** (`all`): each query-key score counts once, divided by the number of combinations.
- **share of |score|**: (`+` minus `−`) of the pair divided by the same summed over the 64 pairs. Every term is non-negative.
- **centred**: the same after subtracting, for each query, the pair's mean score over all keys. Softmax ignores that per-query constant, so this is the part of the score that can move attention.
- **per query**: `a[i] = sum_j p[i,j] c_f[i,j]` is one number per query; the three values are its 5%, median and 95% quantiles across the queries (no pooling over queries).
